# Security Policy

These are Jack Sweeney's personal projects. Thanks for helping keep them safe.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report security issues **privately**. Do not open a public issue or pull request.

- **Email:** [JackHSweeney@protonmail.com](mailto:JackHSweeney@protonmail.com)
- **GitHub:** use **Report a vulnerability** on the repository's **Security** tab.

Include as much of the following as you can:

- The repository and what is affected (file, endpoint, bot, or version)
- Steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept
- The impact you think it has
- How you would like to be credited, if at all

## Scope

- Code in public repositories owned by [Jxck-S](https://github.com/Jxck-S)
- Bots, APIs, and services those projects run

Out of scope:

- TheAirTraffic and Ground Control services. Use the [TheAirTraffic security policy](https://github.com/TheAirTraffic/.github/blob/main/SECURITY.md) for those.
- School coursework repositories and archived or unmaintained projects
- Third-party services and platforms the projects use (report those to the vendor)
- Publicly broadcast aircraft data (ADS-B, MLAT), which is shown by design
- Denial of service, spam, social engineering, and automated scanner reports with no demonstrated impact

## Testing guidelines

- Only test against accounts and data you own.
- Do not access, change, or delete other people's data.
- Do not degrade the projects or their services for others.
- Give a reasonable amount of time to fix an issue before disclosing it publicly.

Good-faith research that follows these guidelines is welcome, and I will not pursue action against it.
